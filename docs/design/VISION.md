# Project vision

## Single-player RTS prototype

The proposed working title is *Lane Command*. In a fixed isometric-like view, the player earns energy, builds barracks, and watches cube units march and fight automatically against a CPU rival. Destroying a base ends the match and the player can restart. The proposed match length after tuning is 2–4 minutes.

## Visual direction and scope

Use flat, solid-color primitives and a minimal HUD readable at typical desktop browser resolutions. Use blue for the player, red for the CPU, and gray for neutral regions. The prototype excludes textures, imported art and meshes, animation, particles, post-processing, audio, shaders, and visual polish. Manual unit orders, fog of war, diplomacy, multiplayer, factions, tech trees, saves, and campaigns are outside scope.
